      Where profiles are stored

      Built-in (locked)
      Ship with Compressarr, so they are always the same and can't be changed or deleted. Duplicate one to make your own.
      Catalog (downloaded) (locked, deletable)
      %AppData%CompressarrProfilescatalog-handbrake.json
      %AppData%CompressarrProfilescatalog-ffmpeg.json
      Profiles you added from the Compressarr catalog (Import > From Catalog). Locked like the built-ins, but you can delete them. Included in the automatic backups and in Export/Import config. HandBrake is handed them in its generated copy together with the built-ins and yours.
      Yours - ffmpeg
      %AppData%\Compressarr\Profiles\ffmpeg-profiles.json
      Your ffmpeg profiles. Included in the automatic backups and in Export/Import config too. ffmpeg reads its profiles straight from here - there is no generated copy.
      Yours - HandBrake
      %AppData%\Compressarr\Profiles\handbrake-profiles.json
      Everything you create, duplicate or import. It is included in Compressarr's automatic backups and in Export/Import config, with the rest of your settings. For every encode Compressarr hands HandBrake its own generated copy (built-ins plus yours), so the HandBrake app and Compressarr never touch each other's files.
