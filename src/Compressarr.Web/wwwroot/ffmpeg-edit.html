Good to know

• Saved to %AppData%\Compressarr\Profiles\ffmpeg-profiles.json - it's in Compressarr's automatic backups with the rest of your settings.

• Names must be unique among the ffmpeg built-ins and your ffmpeg profiles (a HandBrake profile may share a name).

• Renaming a profile updates every ffmpeg lane and queued file that uses it.
